Questions before you start

Does PolicyProof certify compliance?

No. It helps you organise and maintain evidence. It does not certify compliance, guarantee an audit outcome or guarantee that another party will accept your evidence.

Who can access our evidence?

Each organisation has a private workspace; downloads require workspace membership, and files have no public URLs. Workspace members can view and change evidence, so invite only people who should have that access.

What happens when evidence becomes stale?

The overview and review queue show records that need attention. Evidence Readiness reflects the coverage and freshness of what you recorded, not a compliance verdict.

Can I export my work?

PolicyProof generates dated Evidence Packs with a PDF summary, CSV matrix and supporting files.

How does migration work?

You can enter records yourself. Managed migration starts from NZ$350 for suitable source material; complex cleanup or mapping is quoted.
